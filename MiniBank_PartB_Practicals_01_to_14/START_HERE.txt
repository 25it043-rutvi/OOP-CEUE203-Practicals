OOP CEUE203 - MiniBank Part B (Practicals 1-14)

Each Practical-XX folder is an independent checkpoint.
Use JDK 17 or later (records and pattern matching are used).
Open one practical folder at a time in VS Code and follow its README.txt.
Compile/run commands are included in every practical README.

Note:
- Practical 6, 7, 8 and 14 use package folders under src/.
- Practical 14 is a compact integrated capstone implementation; practical-specific requirements are demonstrated in their respective folders.
- Generated data files and compiled .class files are not included.
